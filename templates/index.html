BEHIND THE ATLAS

Every plant has a place.

This interactive GIS atlas explores the plant observations recorded in Mt. Carmel, Bayugan City, Agusan del Sur. The collection contains {{ data.stats.observations }} observations across {{ data.stats.species }} locally named species entries.

Reading the map

Each dot marks a recorded location. Colors identify species, and selecting a plant in the collection shows only its observations. Several records can share a coordinate; use the arrows in the popup to browse them.

About the photographs

Photos are matched to plant species using the supplied folders. They are representative images of that species, rather than verified photos of an individual map point. Species without supplied photos show their recorded information only.

Keeping the source intact

Local and scientific names are shown as recorded in the field workbook. Shared coordinates and observations outside the main area are preserved. The atlas does not infer a surveyed site boundary.
